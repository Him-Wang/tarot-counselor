# Single-Card Deep Read

Ways to work with one card, adapted from the tradition of Mary K. Greer's twenty-one reading methods.

## Methods table (condensed)
1. Describe the card's visible details before interpreting.
2. Name the card's traditional keywords.
3. State the upright meaning in your own words.
4. Consider the reversal.
5. Place the card into the querent's question.
6. Read the card as advice rather than prediction.
7. Identify the element and its quality.
8. Identify the number and its stage.
9. Compare the card to its neighbours if there are any.
10. Notice what image in the card draws your eye first.
11. Imagine yourself inside the scene.
12. Speak for a figure in the card.
13. Turn the figure's statement into a question for the querent.
14. Consider the card's archetypal (major arcana) echo.
15. Name a shadow expression of the card.
16. Name a gifted expression of the card.
17. Ask what the card is asking you to do.
18. Ask what the card is asking you to release.
19. Connect the card to the body or to a felt sense.
20. Link the card to a real current situation.
21. Close with one sentence of integration.

## Five most-used methods
- Describe: "What do you actually see?"
- Traditional meaning: "What has this card come to signify?"
- Personal response: "What does this image stir in you?"
- Advice: "What would acting like this card look like today?"
- One-sentence integration: "So the card is asking me to ___."

## Working with a book meaning
Book meanings are a starting vocabulary, not a script. Translate the printed sentence into the client's language and circumstances.

## Four layers of meaning
1. Literal image and symbol.
2. Traditional card meaning.
3. The querent's personal resonance.
4. The situation's practical demand.

## Three don'ts
- Don't recite a memorised meaning without connecting it to the question.
- Don't let one card become an absolute verdict.
- Don't replace the client's own insight with the reader's authority.
