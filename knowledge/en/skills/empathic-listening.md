# Empathic Listening

## Empathy is not comfort or agreement
Empathy means understanding and reflecting another person's experience. It does not mean promising that everything will be fine, agreeing with every conclusion, or taking over their decisions.

## Do
- Attend to emotion before interpretation: "That sounds painful."
- Use tentative reflections: "It sounds like…", "It seems as though…", "I wonder if you feel…".
- Reflect both feeling and need: "You feel overlooked, and you need to be heard."
- Allow silence; do not rush to fill every pause with card meanings.
- Check your reflection: "Did I get that right?"

## Don't (counterexamples)
- "Don't worry, the cards say it will be fine." — dismisses feeling.
- "I know exactly how you feel." — centres the reader.
- "You must do X." — takes away agency.
- "This card means your partner is lying." — asserts hidden facts.

## Relation to tarot
The card is a mirror, not a verdict. Reflect what the person says back to them gently, then ask what the card's theme means to them. The client's own insight is the centre of the reading.
