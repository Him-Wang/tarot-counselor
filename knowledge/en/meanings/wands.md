# Wands — RWS Meanings

Element: fire. Wands speak of desire, initiative, creativity, and the will to act. Numbered cards show stages of a project; court cards show people or ways of expressing fire.

## Ace of Wands
- Keywords: inspiration, new venture, creative spark
- Core: A new fire is lit. An idea, desire, or project asks to be acted on now.
- Symbolism: a hand emerging from a cloud gripping a sprouting wand, a castle on a hill, a river and trees; raw potential yearning to grow.
- Upright: A surge of energy and a fresh start. Say yes to the idea, but give it a first concrete step.
- Reversed: Delayed spark, scattered desire, or hesitation to begin. The energy is present but not yet directed.

## Two of Wands
- Keywords: planning, choice, ambition, the world in your hands
- Core: You hold power and must decide on a direction. The world is bigger than the plan.
- Symbolism: a figure holding a globe beside a wand fixed to a wall, a castle and sea beyond; dominion and the choice between staying and exploring.
- Upright: Make the plan. A decision about scope, travel, or the next stage. Think widely before committing.
- Reversed: Fear of the unknown, indecision, or a plan that stays on paper. Narrow the choice to one actionable step.

## Three of Wands
- Keywords: expansion, foresight, waiting for results, horizon
- Core: The ships have set sail. You have done the first work and now watch the horizon for what returns.
- Symbolism: a figure on a cliff with three wands, ships sailing away, mountains and sea; expansion and patient expectation.
- Upright: Growth is underway. Broaden the view, trust earlier decisions, and allow time for results.
- Reversed: Delays, limited vision, or disappointment in what has not returned. Revise expectations without abandoning the direction.

## Four of Wands
- Keywords: celebration, stability, homecoming, community
- Core: A milestone is marked. The foundation is stable enough to celebrate.
- Symbolism: four wands with a garland, a archway, dancing figures, a castle; harvest, festivity, and a safe threshold.
- Upright: Celebrate progress. A home, an achievement, or a welcoming community. Pause to enjoy what you built.
- Reversed: Transition, unstable foundations, or a celebration postponed. The milestone may feel less secure than it looks.

## Five of Wands
- Keywords: conflict, competition, differing views, struggle
- Core: Several fires contend. Energy is high but uncoordinated; the clash is part of the work.
- Symbolism: five figures with wands in a mock battle, stormy clouds; competition, conflict, and the testing of skill.
- Upright: Expect friction and lively debate. Compete fairly, or recognise that the disagreement holds useful energy.
- Reversed: Avoided conflict, internal tension, or a dispute that drains rather than sharpens. Step back and choose the real fight.

## Six of Wands
- Keywords: victory, recognition, confidence, public success
- Core: The effort is acknowledged. You ride high after a hard-won result.
- Symbolism: a rider on a horse with a wand crowned by a laurel wreath, a crowd below; public honour and well-earned confidence.
- Upright: Success and recognition. Accept the honour, then decide what to build with the momentum.
- Reversed: Arrogance, lack of recognition, or a victory that feels hollow. Check whether applause matches substance.

## Seven of Wands
- Keywords: defence, resilience, challenge, holding your ground
- Core: You stand on higher ground and defend what you value. Persistence matters more than comfort.
- Symbolism: a figure on a hilltop with a wand, six wands raised below, uneven ground; embattled resolve and energetic defence.
- Upright: Hold your position. Challenges test your commitment; you have the advantage if you stay steady.
- Reversed: Giving up, defensiveness, or exhaustion from constant struggle. Decide what is worth defending and release the rest.

## Eight of Wands
- Keywords: speed, momentum, messages, rapid change
- Core: Things move quickly now. Wands fly through the air; events outpace deliberation.
- Symbolism: eight wands in flight against a clear sky, a landscape and river below; swift movement and unblocked energy.
- Upright: Act fast. News, travel, and rapid progress. Keep direction even while events accelerate.
- Reversed: Delays, impatience, or a sudden stop. Momentum stalls; use the pause to check the aim.

## Nine of Wands
- Keywords: perseverance, vigilance, resilience, last stand
- Core: Wounded but unyielding, you guard the final stretch. The work is nearly done but not yet safe.
- Symbolism: a bandaged figure leaning on a wand, eight wands behind like a fence, a dark cloud; endurance and watchfulness.
- Upright: Keep going. You are tired but stronger than the setback. Protect what you have and finish the task.
- Reversed: Exhaustion, suspicion, or giving up at the last hurdle. Rest, then decide whether the vigil still serves you.

## Ten of Wands
- Keywords: burden, responsibility, overload, carrying too much
- Core: You carry more than is yours. Fire has become obligation; the load blocks the path.
- Symbolism: a figure bowed under ten wands, a village and distant hills, dark sky; heavy responsibility and obscured vision.
- Upright: The load is real but may be self-imposed. Prioritise, delegate, and set something down.
- Reversed: Collapse under pressure, refusal to ask for help, or finally laying the burden down. Release what is not yours to carry.

## Page of Wands
- Keywords: enthusiasm, discovery, messages, creative curiosity
- Core: A youthful fire wants to explore. Curiosity and courage matter more than mastery.
- Symbolism: a young figure in a landscape holding a sprouting wand, distant pyramids and a desert; eager discovery and a message bearer.
- Upright: Good news, a new interest, or a bold first attempt. Let enthusiasm lead you into learning.
- Reversed: Restlessness, false starts, or discouragement. The spark is there; channel it before it fizzles.
- Court perspective: The Page is the beginner's energy — open, excited, and still finding its voice.

## Knight of Wands
- Keywords: action, adventure, passion, impatience
- Core: Fire in motion. The Knight charges toward possibility with courage and little caution.
- Symbolism: a rider on a fiery horse, a flaming cloak, a wand, a desert and pyramids behind; ardour, haste, and charisma.
- Upright: Move decisively. A bold venture, travel, or a charismatic push forward. Check that speed has a destination.
- Reversed: Recklessness, burnout, or a promise that outruns delivery. Slow enough to finish what you start.
- Court perspective: The Knight is desire in motion — inspiring, volatile, and reluctant to wait.

## Queen of Wands
- Keywords: confidence, warmth, creativity, magnetic presence
- Core: The Queen radiates fire with control. She attracts people and makes ideas flourish.
- Symbolism: a queen on a throne with a sunflower, a wand in hand, a black cat at her feet, a lion motif; warmth, sovereignty, and creative charm.
- Upright: Lead with charisma and generosity. Trust your creative power and support others without losing yourself.
- Reversed: Vanity, jealousy, or manipulative warmth. Confidence curdles into domination. Return to genuine generosity.
- Court perspective: The Queen is mature fire — warm, self-possessed, and able to nurture without being consumed.

## King of Wands
- Keywords: leadership, vision, decisiveness,entrepreneurial energy
- Core: The King directs fire toward a goal. Vision becomes strategy and others follow.
- Symbolism: a king on a throne holding a wand, a lion emblem, a red cloak, a desert kingdom; authoritative, creative rule.
- Upright: Take the lead. A visionary decision, a strong leader, or the courage to build something new. Use power to inspire.
- Reversed: Tyranny, impulsiveness, or a leader who cannot follow through. Authority without accountability. Ground the vision.
- Court perspective: The King is fire mastered — the ability to start, inspire, and hold a long purpose.
