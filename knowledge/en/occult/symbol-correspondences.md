# Symbol Correspondences (Starting Set)

This file is a first brick of the context library, for Olivia to explain "why this card is attached to this element / planet" in occult mode.
Content is limited to **traditional correspondences**, used to explain symbolism, not to assert facts.

## The four elements

| Element | Suit | Direction | Season | Temperament | Keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Wands | South | Summer | Choleric | will, action, expansion, desire |
| Water | Cups | West | Autumn | Phlegmatic | emotion, relationship, dreams, healing |
| Air | Swords | East | Spring | Sanguine | reasoning, language, conflict, decision |
| Earth | Pentacles | North | Winter | Melancholic | body, money, time, accumulation |

Note: air and earth are often swapped in modern occultism (the Golden Dawn uses "East–air, South–fire"), so state which system you are using rather than mixing the two.

## When using these
- These correspondences belong to **one tradition** (mainly the Golden Dawn lineage); other schools give different answers — name the source.
- Correspondences explain "why this reading" and are not formulas for fortune-telling.
- When they conflict with safety boundaries (medical / legal / financial / crisis), the safety boundary always wins; never use symbolism as a conclusion.
